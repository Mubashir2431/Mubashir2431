## 💠 About Me

I am a software engineer and technical co-founder studying **Computer Science and Mathematics** (Quantitative Finance concentration) at **Drexel University**. I build production AI systems end to end, from deep learning pipelines for biomedical signals to voice-first agentic products running on AWS.

- 🚛 **Co-Founder and Technical Lead at [feightly.ai](https://portfolio-site-six-ecru.vercel.app)**, a voice-first AI dispatch copilot for owner-operator truck drivers. AWS AIdeas 2026 Semifinalist (Top 300 of 10,000+) and Drexel Startup Fund Finalist.
- 🧠 **Undergraduate AI Researcher (STAR Scholars)** working on fetal EEG signal extraction. I own a two-stage deep learning pipeline built around a 1D U-Net seizure detector trained on the Helsinki neonatal EEG dataset.
- ⚙️ **Full stack by necessity, product-minded by design.** I have shipped mobile clients, serverless backends, geospatial data layers, and multi-agent LLM systems used by 500+ people daily.
- 📈 **Quant trajectory.** I am building toward software engineering in quantitative finance, combining rigorous mathematics with systems that hold up in production.

**Open To:** Software Engineering Co-ops (Quantitative Finance and AI/ML), research collaborations, and early-stage startup conversations.
